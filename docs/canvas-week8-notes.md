## Source qualifications

- Safety slides include historical legal/classification statements. They are study material, not a current operating or legal checklist.
- Autoclave tape records exposure to specified conditions; it alone does not prove that a load is sterile. SAL is probabilistic, and commercial sterility differs from medical-device sterility.
- Microbiome detection is distinguished from colonisation and infection. Contamination, reverse causality and low biomass limit inferences about sterile sites.
- Genotypic transmission is distinguished from phenotype ratios. The assigned clarification paper explains dominance, epistasis, organelle inheritance, maternal effects and meiotic drive. The paper is Strome et al., *GENETICS* 227(3), iyae078 (2024), DOI 10.1093/genetics/iyae078; its figures are used under the paper's CC BY 4.0 licence.
- Chromosomal source errors are qualified: polyploidy means complete extra sets, 45,X is the notation, parental origin is not predetermined, X-inactivation has escape/skewing, and XYY is not an aggression diagnosis. Clinical variability replaces blanket intellect/fertility claims.
- Glucose balance does not double-count gluconeogenesis; hepatocyte entry and insulin regulation are distinct. The source's inevitable-insulin claim is not a universal treatment pathway.
- Benign lesions can grow; familial clustering does not establish a single inherited cause; DCIS progression is not inevitable. Unkeyed breast micrographs are not given fabricated diagnoses.
- Practical tube-number conflicts are identified so controls are recognised by contents. A gel band does not establish a sequence-level diagnosis.

## Current cardiovascular protocol corrections

The 2026–27 handout differs from the older theory/procedure slides: occlusion upper limit 150 mmHg, duration 30 seconds, hot-water upper limit 47°C with 45–46°C target, and the >130 mmHg resting-SBP instruction is to speak to the academic before the occlusion experiment. Four existing card contents receive these known corrections while retaining IDs and all review fields. Explanations identify the newer source and older slide images. Personal edits to those cards are retained. The corrections do not imply that students should carry out the experiments independently.

## Week settings and persistence

`teaching-weeks.js` supplies presentation defaults without touching review dates. Module collections and the lecture selector order by teaching week, with unknown weeks last. Personal week changes are normal synced deck metadata. Clearing a week is explicit; unknown private/imported lectures receive “Week not set” rather than a guessed schedule. The two injury parts keep independent week preferences through their existing shared-source storage model.

New bundled lectures seed only when missing. Existing cards, progress, personal modules and trash choices remain intact. The targeted practical corrections change known card content without deleting cards, replacing IDs or resetting reviews.
