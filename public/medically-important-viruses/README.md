Explanations preserve these distinctions:

- Acute does not mean necessarily mild or invariably uncomplicated. Latency is distinct from continuing symptomatic disease.
- HSV types are not restricted exclusively to oral versus genital sites. Antivirals suppress active replication rather than eradicating latency.
- Antigenic shift concerns influenza A and can contribute to pandemics; it does not guarantee pandemic spread. Reduced severity and prevention of infection are different vaccine outcomes.
- HIV infection is not synonymous with AIDS. Sustained undetectable viral load on effective ART prevents sexual transmission; this is not a cure or a claim about every route.
- HPV positivity is not a cancer diagnosis, and the progression pathway is not inevitable. The slides do not give HPV protein targets, quantitative individual cancer risks, virus-specific diagnostic windows or treatment regimens, so none are invented.
