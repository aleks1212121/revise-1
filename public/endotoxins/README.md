Source clarifications:

- True endotoxin is LPS/LOS. Gram-positive lipoteichoic acid and muramyl peptides are not chemically identical endotoxins, despite the source’s endotoxin-like comparison.
- Living bacteria release endotoxin, including in outer-membrane vesicles; activation is not restricted absolutely to a cell-free state. Lysis-associated release does not imply indicated antibiotics should be withheld.
- Lipid A is the principal toxic moiety. O-antigen variation influences antigenicity/serotypes; it is not the sole determinant of toxicity. Core composition varies; heptoses are seven-carbon sugars and Kdo is eight-carbon, correcting the seven-carbon Kdo label.
- LOS lacks the long O-antigen repeat chain. Structures vary within genera; the source’s Bordetella/Bacteroides grouping is not memorised as a universal rule. Aggregation affects presentation but smaller micelles are not a universal size-only toxicity law.
- CD14 assists delivery to the TLR4–MD-2 system; soluble CD14 can support cells with little membrane CD14. The schematic is simplified, not a complete cell-specific signalling map.
- Sepsis is infection-related life-threatening organ dysfunction, not “large LPS amount” or fever alone. Non-LPS pathogens can cause sepsis. Patient outcomes are possible complications, not inevitable consequences of carriage or any ExPEC infection.
- Chlamydial LPS structural immunogenicity is not explained simply by lack of peptidoglycan. Chlamydia has limited/division-associated peptidoglycan, rather than the source’s implied interchangeable LPS/peptidoglycan chemistry.
- C. jejuni ganglioside mimicry concerns LOS oligosaccharide structures, not merely lipid A. Guillain–Barré syndrome is a possible minority post-infectious complication; the epidural-abscess caption is not its definition.
- C5a is a major neutrophil chemoattractant; C3a is chiefly an anaphylatoxin rather than an equivalent. C3bBb is alternative C3 convertase, stabilised by properdin. The listed LPS/factors are not one simple binding receptor complex.
- Individual prostaglandins/leukotrienes have different actions. The broad source grouping must not be read as every molecule producing the same vascular effect.
- Prothrombinase uses Xa/Va plus calcium/phospholipid surfaces. VIIIa acts upstream with IXa in intrinsic tenase; it is not part of the stated Xa–V–VIII complex. Sepsis coagulopathy prominently involves tissue-factor signalling as well as interactions not captured by the slide’s XII-first model.
- DIC can cause microvascular ischaemia and bleeding from consumption. The source does not supply a complete diagnostic or treatment algorithm, so none is invented. No covered photograph is used to guess clinical signs.
