Important source clarifications:

- Chemokines are a cytokine subset; GM-CSF/G-CSF are colony-stimulating cytokines rather than chemokines. IL-10/TGF-β and other context-dependent signals should not all be labelled simply pro-inflammatory. T-cell pathway figures are simplified, not exclusive rules or differentiation protocols.
- Platelets participate but are not white blood cells. Innate and adaptive responses interact; chronic inflammation is not exclusively adaptive.
- Vasodilation increases flow initially. Fluid loss and concentration of cells contribute to later stasis, so vasodilation alone is not equated immediately with stasis. Exudate versus transudate refers to permeability/protein content and mechanisms, not merely any fluid outside a vessel.
- C5a is a major neutrophil chemoattractant; C3a is chiefly an anaphylatoxin, not an equivalent chemotactic signal. Rolling uses selectin interactions; firm adhesion uses activated integrins with endothelial ligands such as ICAM/VCAM, correcting the slide's integrin–selectin statement.
- The vascular-permeability activity has seven arrows but asks for five mechanisms and gives no complete key. Recognised mechanisms are explained without guessing precise numbered assignments from its low-detail diagram.
- NET trapping differs from intracellular phagocytosis. Pus is neutrophil-rich but also contains fluid, debris and often microbes. Abscess organisation can hinder clearance, without proving antibiotics can never enter or neighbouring healthy cells simply form an absolute wall.
- Repair phases/times overlap and vary. Granulation tissue is new vascular/fibroblast-rich repair tissue, not a granuloma. Growth-factor actions are context dependent; TGF-β's in-vitro antiproliferative effects do not preclude fibrogenesis.
- A granuloma is an organised macrophage-rich response, without a universal five-cell/1–2 mm definition. Sarcoidosis is not an established infection by a named microorganism. Histology alone is not a unique organism diagnosis. One TB architecture image is explicitly an animal-model image.
- Fever is regulated thermoregulatory signalling; not all pathogens grow best below 37°C and not every fever is benign. Sepsis means infection-related life-threatening organ dysfunction, rather than any systemic inflammatory response. The source provides no complete diagnostic/treatment criteria.
- Tumour/repair analogies and inflammatory cancer associations are not deterministic predictions. The older Th1/Th2 tumour diagram is simplified; cytokines do not all belong exclusively in one cell-source/effect category.
- Crohn's granulomas may be absent; disease extent and cancer risks vary. Adipose signalling and IL-6/SOCS3 diagrams describe models/context, not every physiological action or individual outcome. The source's simplistic LDL annotation is not promoted into a universal causal statement.
