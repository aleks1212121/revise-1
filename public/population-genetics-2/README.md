Clarifications:

- Independent assortment requires unlinked/effectively unlinked loci. Complete dominance does not cover all inheritance, and does not determine commonness or fitness. Red hair is a simplified recessive example; actual hair colour is genetically more complex.
- Direct allele counting does not require HWE. The p/q form uses two alleles; HWE can be generalised. Non-overlapping generations and other introductory model settings should not be treated as making all human applications invalid.
- Non-random mating can alter genotype proportions without directly changing allele frequencies. Recombination reshuffles haplotypes and LD rather than directly changing a single-locus allele frequency. Neither HWE agreement nor departure uniquely diagnoses an evolutionary force.
- CCR5 Δ32 protection concerns CCR5-tropic HIV-1; the source's unqualified resistance label is not universal immunity. Clinical progression associations are not individual guarantees.
- Longer histories and out-of-Africa founder effects help explain diversity patterns, with substantial heterogeneity among African populations. Lower LD and higher sequence diversity are distinct. The source's subset diagram is not a literal claim about every modern variant after later mutations, migration and archaic admixture.
- Genetic ancestry/population structure do not map neatly to fixed social racial categories. Ethnolinguistic labels describe sampling/social contexts, not uniform closed genetic groups. The diversity figure is not a hierarchy of human worth, health or fitness.
