Source clarifications:

- The entire first trimester is not the embryonic period. Major organogenesis is approximately weeks 3–8 post-fertilisation; the fetal period begins around post-fertilisation week 9. Gestational age is generally about two weeks ahead. The source uses timing conventions inconsistently, so unqualified exact gestational-week questions are avoided.
- The sperm's acrosomal reaction, penetration of coverings and gamete-membrane fusion are distinct. The first sperm arriving need not fertilise; a fixed number of sperm reaching the tube is not tested as universal.
- Morula cell counts are approximate. The embryo-proper and fetal placental lineages are separated; trophoblast is not the entire placenta. Inner-cell-mass/epiblast pluripotency is distinguished from totipotency.
- Major folding occurs during week 4 and extraembryonic structures do not all first form at that point. Tissue derivatives refer to lineages, not a single origin for every component of an organ. Shared animal developmental processes do not erase species differences.
- Blaschko's lines reflect developmental skin-cell mosaic/clonal patterns, not simply neural-crest migration.
- Common XX/XY patterns are not exceptionless phenotype rules. X inactivation is largely somatic and incomplete for some genes; skewing and cell-type differences exist. The disease examples chiefly illustrate X-linked recessive inheritance, not all X-linked disorders.
- SRY acts through a wider regulatory network and downstream signalling/tissue responsiveness matters. Klinefelter/Turner examples retain variable/mosaic clinical possibilities rather than universal phenotype predictions.
- AMH specifically promotes Müllerian regression; it does not attack every female-associated structure. Testosterone's Wolffian effects are distinguished from DHT-dependent external/prostate differentiation. The prostate is not labelled as a Wolffian derivative.
- The external-genital diagram's broad shaft descriptions are qualified: labioscrotal swellings chiefly map to labia majora/scrotum, while urogenital folds make distinct contributions. The exact fraction assigned to vaginal origins is not memorised as invariant.
