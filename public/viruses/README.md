# Revisiting Microbes — Viruses

A reviewed LS5008 deck from the uploaded 43-slide lecture by Dr Shadia Omar. The app adds it automatically under Infection and Immunity, without replacing existing decks or study progress.

200 focused cloze cards include 14 visual recall questions, 13 supporting figure panels and all 43 original slide pictures. Topics cover viral discovery, structure, genomes and Baltimore classification, entry and replication, bacteriophages, zoonosis, latency, vaccines, HIV, prions and viroids. Prompt crops remove answer headings where relevant; full source slides appear after reveal and remain in Slide reference.

Cards clarify source oversimplifications: mature HIV has a conical core; VZV latency uses episomal DNA rather than obligatory chromosome integration; HPV integration is not required for every infection; specialised hepatitis C culture systems now exist; the exact SARS-CoV-2 emergence pathway is unresolved. Baltimore groups I and VII explicitly complete the five groups shown on slide 22. Source pictures are unchanged. Unavailable embedded video contents are not invented. Detailed editorial notes are included in deck.json.

Images are extracted from the supplied lecture and attributed by slide number. No paid AI service or backend is required to study this deck. New cards use the existing review schedule and account sync.

Validation: `npm test` checks cloze answers, source/media completeness, distinct prompt/answer images and editorial corrections. Against the built preview on port 4189, `npm run test:viruses:browser` checks automatic loading, module grouping, all visual images, supporting diagrams, phone grading, reload persistence and retention of existing microorganism reviews.
